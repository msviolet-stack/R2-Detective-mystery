The Vanishing Founder’s Key

During preparations for the Institute's Open Night, the ceremonial Founder’s Key disappears. Four people were moving through the building. Each visited a different location, carried a different unusual object, and had a different reason for being there.

One suspect is lying. Work out the complete pattern to discover who took the key.

Case Brief

At 7:19 PM, curator Dr Vale opened the Founder’s Cabinet and discovered that the brass ceremonial key was gone. The cabinet was not forced.

Investigators know that the four suspects each went to a different location, carried a different object, and had a different motive.

Your challenge: determine each person's location, object and motive. Then identify the one person whose statement cannot be true.

Guest Researcher

Important Rule

Each location, object and motive belongs to exactly one person.

Exactly one statement is false.

Clues & Evidence

1. The person in the Glasshouse carried the Blue Lantern.
2. Mina was preparing a surprise, but she did not visit the Observatory.
3. The person carrying the Tool Case was repairing a display.
4. Felix did not carry the Map Tube or Blue Lantern.
5. The Archive Vault visitor was researching the founder.
6. Clara carried the Map Tube.
7. Owen was not in the Clock Tower or Glasshouse.
8. A circular mark on the empty key stand reacts strongly to iron filings. Investigators believe a magnet was placed there.
9. The person who wanted to keep the key “safe” went to the Clock Tower.
10. The Blue Lantern was signed out by someone other than Clara.

Teacher Solution

Felix Rowan took the key. He went to the Clock Tower, carried the Brass Magnet, and claimed he wanted to keep the key “safe”. Felix is the one lying when he says he did not go to the Clock Tower.

Complete matching: Clara → Archive Vault → Map Tube → research founder. Owen → Observatory → Tool Case → repair display. Mina → Glasshouse → Blue Lantern → prepare surprise. Felix → Clock Tower → Brass Magnet → keep key “safe”.

The magnet evidence explains how the key could be lifted from its stand without forcing the cabinet mechanism during a brief period when the cabinet was open for exhibit setup.
